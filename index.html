The guidelines (what you're going for, not what to say)
  1. Walk in like a neighbor.Find the owner, say who you are and that you're local, and promise it's quick.
  2. Get them talking.How many jobs a week, and what a normal job is worth. Get their numbers, not yours.
  3. Do the math with their numbers.One lost job a week times 52. Say it slowly, then go quiet.
  4. Show, don't tell.Demo built: "I'm not here to pitch you a website. I'm here to show you what we already built for you." Hand over the phone. No demo: have them pull up their site, or ask where people go when they Google them.
  5. The offer, like you're telling a friend.Own it for $800 and it's on you if it breaks, or $599 and $149 a month and you handle everything.
  6. Ask plainly."Want me to put it up this week?" Then stop talking.
  7. Leave with something.A yes, their cell, or a time to come back. Then ask who else you should talk to.

Objections: agree first, answer in a sentence or two, bring it back to their money, ask again. No jargon.
